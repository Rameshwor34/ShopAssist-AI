SHOPASSIST AI - CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT SHOPASSIST

ShopAssist is an AI-powered e-commerce customer support platform. Customers can ask questions about orders, shipping, returns, refunds, payments, account access, and products.

ORDER SUPPORT

Customers can check their order status using their order number from the Orders section of their account.

Order statuses include:
- Processing: the order has been received and is being prepared.
- Shipped: the package has left the fulfillment center.
- Out for delivery: the package is with the delivery carrier.
- Delivered: the carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before completion.

If an order remains in processing for an unusually long time, customers should contact customer support with their order number.

SHIPPING

Standard shipping normally takes 3-5 business days after an order has been dispatched.

Express shipping normally takes 1-2 business days after dispatch.

Shipping estimates may be affected by weekends, public holidays, weather, carrier delays, or incorrect delivery information.

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking number provided in their shipping confirmation.

If tracking information has not updated for more than 48 hours, customers should contact support.

RETURNS

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery for eligible products.

Products should normally be unused and returned in their original condition and packaging.

Some products may be excluded from the standard return policy, including personalized products, certain hygiene-sensitive products, and final-sale items.

Customers should initiate a return through the Returns section of their account.

REFUNDS

After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is normally processed within 5-7 business days.

The time for the refund to appear in a customer's bank account or card account can depend on the payment provider.

If a refund has been marked as completed but has not appeared after the expected processing period, the customer should contact support.

CANCELLATIONS

Customers can request cancellation while an order is still in the processing stage.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible. In that situation, the customer may need to use the normal return process after delivery.

PAYMENTS

ShopAssist supports major debit and credit cards and other payment methods displayed during checkout.

If a payment fails, customers should verify their billing information and available funds, then retry the payment.

Customers should never send complete card numbers, passwords, CVV codes, or other sensitive payment credentials through customer support chat.

ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Customers can reset a forgotten password through the password reset option on the login page.

If an account appears compromised, the customer should immediately change the password and contact customer support.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Product availability depends on current inventory.

If a product is out of stock, customers can check the product page later for updated availability.

Product specifications, dimensions, compatibility, and included accessories should be verified from the product listing before purchase.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For order-specific problems, customers should provide their order number but should not provide passwords, complete payment card numbers, or security codes.

ShopAssist AI should answer general questions using the knowledge base and should avoid inventing policies that are not supported by the retrieved documents.

If the knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer a question confidently, the assistant should clearly state that additional information or human support may be required.
